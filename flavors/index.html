What is a food flavors forecast?

A food flavors forecast is a projection of which flavors will grow or decline across menus, retail products and consumer conversation in a coming period, based on each flavor's current lifecycle stage and year over year growth rather than editorial opinion.

Quick answer

The 2027 food flavors forecast names matcha the leading flavor, up 26.1 percent year over year with growing menu share, while strawberry matcha (+16.4 percent) and texture formats like cold foam (+57 percent) emerge behind it. Ube, yuzu, green tea and boba decline, marking the end of the previous specialty flavor cycle.

Matcha grew 26.1 percent year over year in the broad US flavor conversation as of July 2026. Inside the specialty flavor conversation it holds a 77 percent social share on roughly 532,000 posts, with an 8.5 percent menu share. No other flavor in the dataset combines that scale with that rate of growth.

The ecosystem is growing faster than the base. Strawberry matcha grew 16.4 percent from an emerging base, cold foam grew 57 percent and banana pairings grew 36.8 percent over the same period. Each of those is a format or a pairing rather than a new flavor, which is where the 2027 headroom sits.

The caution is the default serve. Matcha latte as a standalone term already declines 17.3 percent year over year, signaling that consumers have moved past the 2024 era default. Implication: enter matcha with a differentiated format or pairing, not a me too latte.

Flavor trends 2027 by category

Beverage flavor trends 2027

Matcha formats lead the 2027 beverage flavor call, supported by cold foam texture at 57 percent year over year growth. Latte grew 9.9 percent at a 9.1 percent US menu share, and soda remains trending at a 40.8 percent menu share, the widest available distribution for a new serve. The pattern across all three is that format is carrying the flavor rather than the reverse. Action: lead the 2027 beverage line with a texture claim and attach the flavor to it.

Soda and soft drink flavor trends 2027

Soda holds a 40.8 percent US menu share and sits in the trending lifecycle stage as of July 2026, which puts it among the highest reach categories in the dataset. The flavor story in soda is currently a format story, driven by texture, carbonation level and serve rather than by novel flavor names. Flavor specific soda data will be added to this section as it lands, and no flavor level soda figures are claimed here. Action: test format variants on existing flavors before committing to a new soda flavor SKU.

Snack flavor trends 2027

Jalapeno holds a 10.7 percent US menu share and sits in the mature stage, so heat is table stakes rather than a differentiator in 2027. The movement is in texture: the creamy need grew 11.2 percent and the soft need grew 10.2 percent year over year in US food conversation as of July 2026. That points snack flavor innovation toward texture pairings on established heat and savory profiles. Action: pair a mature flavor with a growing texture claim rather than launching an unfamiliar flavor alone.

Ice cream and dessert flavor trends 2027

Pistachio is mature at 2.8 percent year over year growth and tiramisu is stable at 0.5 percent, so both hold their place without justifying new investment. Matcha desserts are the growth entry point for 2027, carried by the 26.1 percent growth in the base flavor. Ube, down 21.6 percent, and mochi, down 26.5 percent, are in decline and should release freezer and menu space. Action: fund a matcha dessert line from space currently held by the declining specialty flavors.

Energy drink flavor trends 2027

The energy consumer need grew 11.8 percent year over year in US food conversation as of July 2026, at the same time as the generic healthy claim fell 38.5 percent. Read directionally, that is demand moving from an umbrella wellness promise toward a named functional outcome. This section reports the functional need rather than flavor level energy drink figures, which are not present in the July 2026 dataset. Action: lead energy positioning with the functional outcome and treat flavor as the secondary choice.

In 2027 texture claims differentiate faster than new flavor introductions. Flavor cycles are compressing, with the previous specialty cycle losing between 20 and 41 percent of its conversation in a single year, while texture formats extend the life of a flavor already in market.   • Dessert teams: use soft and rich texture language to extend matcha beyond its first season.

Frequently asked questions

What is the flavor of the year for 2027?

Matcha is the flavor of the year for 2027. Matcha grew 26.1 percent year over year in the broad US flavor conversation as of July 2026 and holds an 8.5 percent menu share within the specialty conversation, where it accounts for a 77 percent social share on roughly 532,000 posts. The caveat is format: matcha latte as a standalone term declined 17.3 percent, so growth sits in newer serves.

What flavors are trending for 2027?

The flavors trending for 2027 are matcha, up 26.1 percent year over year, and strawberry matcha, up 16.4 percent from a small emerging base. Cold foam textures grew 57 percent, the fastest single signal in the flavor dataset. Banana pairings grew 36.8 percent. All four are measured in US social food conversation as of July 2026 and all four carry a grow call.

What flavors are going out of style in 2027?

The flavors going out of style in 2027 are green tea, down 40.8 percent year over year, boba, down 31.7 percent, yuzu, down 28.8 percent, mochi, down 26.5 percent, ube, down 21.6 percent, and lavender, down 20.8 percent. All six are measured in US specialty flavor conversation as of July 2026 and all six carry an exit call in this forecast.

What are the beverage flavor trends for 2027?

The beverage flavor trends for 2027 are matcha formats, cold foam texture and continued latte growth. Latte grew 9.9 percent year over year at a 9.1 percent US menu share, while cold foam grew 57 percent inside the specialty beverage conversation. Soda remains trending at a 40.8 percent menu share, which makes it the largest available surface for a texture led or format led beverage launch.

How is a flavor forecast different from a flavor trend list?

A flavor trend list names what is popular now, usually ranked by a single growth figure. A flavor forecast assigns each flavor a lifecycle stage and projects where it lands next year, which is why a fast growing flavor can still receive an exit call if it is growing off a declining base. The forecast frame is what separates a hold call from a grow call. See how these forecasts are built.

Is matcha still trending in 2027?

Matcha is still trending going into 2027, with nuance. The base flavor grew 26.1 percent year over year in US social food conversation as of July 2026, and extensions such as strawberry matcha grew 16.4 percent. The default 2024 era serve is fading though, with matcha latte as a standalone term down 17.3 percent, so the growth sits in new formats and pairings rather than the familiar latte.
